**Asking Motet**

Motet keeps work moving between this project's agents. You can ask it with ask_motet when:

- You sent a message or handed off work, your next step depends on the answer, and it hasn't come. For example: “Did Bram get my message about the export format, and has he started?”
- You can't reach another agent.
- You're blocked and the plan doesn't say what to do.
- You need the owner to decide something.

Ask once. Then carry on with other work and collect Motet's answer at your next checkpoint. Don't send your original message again: Motet checks whether it arrived and delivers it again only if it didn't.

Don't ask Motet for what you can check yourself, to pass on a message you can send, or to approve anything. Motet's answer is information, not the owner's approval.

If Motet doesn't answer, it may be down. Write “Note for Motet:” and your question in your own chat, then carry on. Motet picks it up when it is back.
