**Name:** Morning brief

**Use when:** The owner opens Motet for the first time after being away overnight. If they haven't by the set time, the brief waits for them, and the day's goals that start at the set time start. Not while the owner has turned the brief off in Settings: then goal suggestions go to Next up as one proposal, warnings as one folded line, and nothing starts at the set time without the owner's yes.

**Reads:** a fresh project sweep; the Agenda (goals, promises, standing yeses); the Waiting on you list; last night's end-of-day report; whether backups are on.

### Steps

1. Run a fresh project sweep. Never build a brief from memory.
2. Open with what needs the owner, from Waiting on you: most important first, each ready for a one-word answer.
3. Then what happened since the owner left, project by project: what finished, what failed, who is stuck. A line each.
4. Then suggested goals for today, for the teams where goals would help: one or two concrete outcomes, each with what it is based on and its done line. Use discernment: a quiet project or a team already on track can be left alone.
   - A team working toward a project goal the owner set: mark that its goals start at the set time if the owner doesn't answer.
   - A team between project goals: say it is waiting, and list the project goals its manager suggests for the owner to choose from.
   - A team whose project couldn't be fully read: no suggestion, and say which part couldn't be read.
5. Then promises due today, and any that are slipping.
6. Then stale Agenda items, those with no progress by their check-by date, each as “Keep, change, or drop?” with its owner and what it was for.
7. End with warnings: backups off, spending heading for the daily cap, a goal that won't make its date at this pace.
8. Let the owner confirm or edit the goals in one go. For each goal confirmed, or started at the set time, run Kick off a goal.

### Never

- Suggest a goal without a fresh sweep.
- Invent goals to fill the brief. Saying nothing about a team is fine.
- Start a team that is between project goals, or set a project goal.
- Bury a decision the owner needs to make under the news.

### For example

Two things need you: Ada's export button is ready to merge, and Cleo asks which payment provider to use. Overnight, Bram finished the settings screen; one check failed and is being fixed. Today the recipe team aims to ship the export button and start the meal planner; these start at ten if you don't answer. The garden team is between goals; its manager suggests three to choose from.

This method grants no new authority. What Motet may do on its own is set by RULES.md and the permission table.
