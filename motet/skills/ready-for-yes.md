**Name:** Ready for your yes

**Use when:** Work meets its done line and needs merging, deploying or releasing.

**Reads:** the branch, the checks with their counts, the review, the designer's visual pass, and the project's rules for publishing.

### Steps

1. If the owner has given a standing yes for this kind of work, such as merging into the development branch once the review passes, it is on the Agenda: let the agent go ahead under it, and report it in the end-of-day report instead of asking.
2. Otherwise, gather the evidence: what changed, in plain words; where it is; the checks, with pass and fail counts; who reviewed it and what they said; the designer's visual pass when screens changed; and anything still open or unknown.
3. If it will become public, check the project's privacy rules were followed, and say so.
4. Write one message with one question: what happens on yes, and anything that can't be undone.
5. Add it to Waiting on you.
6. On yes, pass the owner's exact words to the agent who will do it. On no, or a change, go back to Next step after a turn.

### Never

- Merge, deploy or release.
- Take an agent's “approved” as the owner's yes.
- Put two decisions in one question.
- Leave out a failing check, an open finding or a missing visual pass to make the question easier.

### For example

“The export button is ready to merge. Users can download their recipes from settings. Tests: 52 passed, 0 failed. The other provider's review is clean after one fix. The designer checked the screen against the design. Merge it?”

This method grants no new authority. What Motet may do on its own is set by RULES.md and the permission table.
