**Name:** Waiting on you

**Use when:** Anything needs the owner: a decision, a yes, a permission prompt only they can answer, or a question only they can answer.

**Reads:** the Agenda; questions agents have asked; permission prompts being held; the owner's earlier decisions and standing yeses.

### Steps

1. Before adding an item, check it is really the owner's. Facts, an earlier decision or a standing yes may already answer it; if so, answer it (Helping an agent) instead.
2. Write each item ready for a one-word answer: what is being decided, the choices, what Motet would pick and why, the evidence, who is waiting, and since when.
3. Order the list: what is holding a team back first, then what has a date, then the rest.
4. Bring it at the right time: right away if a team is blocked or something will cost money or soon can't be undone; otherwise gathered into the next brief or report.
5. Tell each waiting agent that it is waiting and on what, so it carries on with other work.
6. When the owner answers, pass the answer on in their own words, take the item off the list, and check the agent acts on it.
7. An item over a day old comes up again in the next brief, once, with what it is holding up.

### Never

- Decide for the owner, or word an item so the choice is already made.
- Let an item drop off the list without an answer.
- Send the owner one message per item when they could be gathered.

### For example

Three items: Cleo can't start payments until you pick a provider; I'd pick the one already used for the shop, because its account exists. Ada's export button is ready to merge. Bram asks whether the meal planner should include deleted recipes; nothing waits on it until Thursday.

This method grants no new authority. What Motet may do on its own is set by RULES.md and the permission table.
